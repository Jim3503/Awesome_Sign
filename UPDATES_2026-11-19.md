## Comparison rules

1. Keep ISLR, CSLR, video-to-text SLT, text-to-gloss and SLP results separate.
2. Record signer independence, target-sentence overlap, external pretraining, input modality and supervision before comparing scores.
3. For retrieval systems, distinguish a training-only gallery from any use of held-out data and disclose deduplication.
4. Report semantic faithfulness and community/user evaluation alongside text-overlap scores where appropriate.
5. Do not infer that text-free pretraining makes the downstream translation pipeline unsupervised, or that multilingual spoken-text coverage equals multilingual sign coverage.
